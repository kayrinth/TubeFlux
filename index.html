TubeFlux — paste one YouTube or YouTube Music playlist link and the whole thing comes back as tagged MP3 or MP4.

  • One link, the whole playlist.
  • MP3 or MP4, your choice.
  • Title, artist and cover come embedded.
  • 320 kbps or 1080p.
  • One dead video stops nothing.
  • It all comes back as one ZIP.

Paste one playlist link. TubeFlux reads the whole list first, downloads track by track, then hands it back as a tagged MP3 or MP4 — title, artist and cover already embedded in the file.

Notice

  1. 01

    This tool is for material you have the right to download: your own work, openly licensed releases, and the public domain.

  2. 02

    Copying copyrighted music breaks YouTube’s Terms of Service. That call is yours, not this tool’s.

  3. 03

    TubeFlux never asks you to sign in and never touches your YouTube account.

  4. 04

    Downloads stay on the machine running TubeFlux, one folder per job, and are never cleaned up for you.

Get it

Node 18 or newer is all it asks for: ffmpeg ships with the package, and yt-dlp is fetched once if you do not already have it.

Install once 
$ npm i -g tubeflux

Then type tubeflux from any folder. On macOS and Linux npm's global folder usually belongs to root, so add sudo if the install is refused.

Or run it without installing 
$ npx tubeflux

Nothing lands on your system and no permission is ever needed. Put npx in front of any command below.

  1. 01
    Answer the menu

    It asks what you want — music or video — and at what quality. Nothing is downloaded until you have answered.

  2. 02
    Paste the link

    Any playlist or single video from YouTube or YouTube Music. TubeFlux reads the whole list first, so you see every track before the first byte moves.

  3. 03
    Take your files

    They land in a downloads folder beside wherever you ran the command, one folder per job, titles and covers already in the files.

In a hurry: tubeflux "<link>" -f audio -q 320 skips the menu. Prefer a browser? tubeflux --serve opens the same board on your own machine.

Everything stays on your machine.

No account, no upload, no server in the middle. TubeFlux runs where you run it, and the files never leave.
